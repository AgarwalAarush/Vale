# Independent concept review

Reviewer: /root/compact_companion_design_review. Read-only assignment.
Inspected all three final generated candidate images directly on 2026-10-20.
This is a recommendation about visual explorations, not owner approval,
native runtime acceptance or a Factory production review.

Reading Lens is the strongest foundation. It makes the desktop context
believable, gives the screenshot a clear identity and keeps the reply concise.
Its short takeaway, explanation and equation provide the clearest hierarchy.
Smoke supplies the strongest dark material direction.

- Lens: tighten attachment strip, header and composer spacing. Keep the source
  removable in the reply state. Define a visible Send control for follow-up
  entry, as well as native keyboard submission.
- Smoke: keep the contrast and depth; reduce waveform scale, omit the generated
  extra paragraph and keep system type rather than the generated serif answer.
- Pearl: approachable and explicit context. Remove duplicated explanation,
  tighten equation spacing and increase follow-up placeholder contrast.
- Across concepts: keep the top-right corner exactly fixed, maintain input
  focus and allow immediate Escape cancellation. Generated optical effects
  are targets rather than proof of native behavior on arbitrary backgrounds.

Recommendation: Lens structure and concise answer discipline, with Pearl/light
and Smoke/dark material treatments. A further broad concept round is unnecessary.

and vale-chat.png; Stratum/app/overview.png; and Freya AGENTS.md/src/styles.css.
